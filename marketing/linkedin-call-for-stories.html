First 210 characters

Before you're an expert, you're a human.

That's the line Humans of ERP started from. Sixty-two stories later, it still holds.

We built the site to spotlight the people behind Australia's ERP work — the consultant who stayed late, the change lead who held a room together, the support analyst nobody thanked. Not the vendor logos. Not the methodology. The people.

Here's what surprised me reading all of them back: the themes that come up most often aren't technical. Leadership. Courage. Curiosity. Adapting. People first. Resilience. Almost nobody's best story is about a data migration — it's about what the migration cost them, or taught them, or who got them through it.

So we're opening the door wider.

If you work in ERP, we'd like to hear from you. Consultant, change lead, architect, functional analyst, PM, tester, support, first-year graduate — it doesn't matter where you sit. Three questions, that's the whole thing:

What shaped you? What have you overcome? What do you stand for?

You don't need a dramatic turnaround or a title that impresses anyone. You need a real answer.

Submit yours at humansoferp.com — link in the comments.

And if someone came to mind while you were reading this, tag them below. That's how a community like this actually grows.
