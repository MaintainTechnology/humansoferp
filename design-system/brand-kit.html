08 — Motion

Motion vocabulary

Button hover/press: 200ms ease colors, transform 160ms cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1), press scale(0.97)
CTA arrow nudge on hover: translateX(3px) — pointer devices only
Card hover lift: translateY(-4px) over 250ms ease-out-strong
Menu panel slide-in: 400ms cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1), overlay fade 350ms
Sticky header frost: 0.5s ease-in-out, logo shrinks to 150px after 100px scroll
Home marquee: translateX(0 → −50%) over 150s linear infinite, pauses on hover
All motion honors prefers-reduced-motion
